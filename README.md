The mod is currently under active development, and, subject to the progress of implementation and internal testing, its first public release may be made available around the middle of the summer vacation period in China. **MoreRealisticGeneEditing (MRGE)** is a science-oriented educational and gameplay-expansion mod designed for **Minecraft 26.1 / Fabric / Java 25**, initiated and developed by a group of university students with academic backgrounds in biology, chemistry, computer science, and related disciplines. Motivated by a shared intellectual commitment to gene editing, cellular therapy, and life-science education, the development team seeks to employ Minecraft as an accessible, participatory, and highly interactive medium through which the general public may be introduced to the foundational concepts of contemporary genetic engineering and medical biotechnology.

Drawing conceptual inspiration from real-world CAR-T cell therapy while deliberately avoiding the replication of any actionable biomedical procedure, MRGE transforms a series of abstracted biotechnological elements—including an automated cell processing system, CO₂ cylinders, disposable TS520 tubing sets, a Day 0–11 Activity Matrix workflow, resource management, machine-state transitions, failure-condition adjudication, and embedded ethical guidance—into a coherent set of playable Minecraft mechanics. Within this system, players are required to craft and deploy a CliniMACS Prodigy-inspired device, install disposable tubing, connect an external gas supply, and introduce abstracted in-game materials such as leukopaks, culture media, buffer solution, magnetic beads, activators, and viral vectors; through the graphical user interface, they monitor parameters such as temperature, pH, cell count, and process stage progression, ultimately producing CAR-T formulations that are represented exclusively as in-game potion or buff items and are not associated with any real-world clinical application.

The mod places particular emphasis on scientific literacy, medical ethics, and practical server-side deployability. It does **not** provide, imply, or facilitate instructions for real-world gene editing, cell culture, viral manipulation, or clinical practice. Instead, through persistent ethical notices, deliberately abstracted resources, a modern laboratory-inspired visual language, and configurable process durations, MRGE aims to enable players to understand, within clearly delineated safety boundaries, the equipment, consumables, environmental control systems, quality-management logic, and ethics-oriented principles that underlie complex biotechnological workflows and the broader ideal of technology directed toward human welfare.
